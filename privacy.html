Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026 · Applies to the ClipPilot app and its connections to TikTok and YouTube

In short

  • ClipPilot runs on the operator’s own computer. There is no ClipPilot server, account system or database in the cloud.
  • Videos, clips, transcripts and login tokens stay on that computer.
  • Data leaves the computer only when you use a feature that needs another service: posting to TikTok or YouTube, or the optional AI features.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

1. Who we are

ClipPilot is a video-clipping and publishing tool operated by the owner of the Clip Pilot channel (“we”, “us”). It turns long videos that the user owns or has permission to use into short clips, and publishes clips the user approves to the user’s own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is used by its operator and a small number of invited collaborators; it is not offered to the general public.

2. Information ClipPilot uses

Content you add

The video files you choose, the transcripts ClipPilot creates from them, and the clips, titles, descriptions, hashtags and caption edits you make. These are stored only on the computer running ClipPilot.

Information from TikTok, when you connect a TikTok account

Permission (scope)What ClipPilot reads or doesWhy
user.info.basicYour TikTok open ID, display name, username and avatarTo show which account a clip will be posted to
video.publishPosts a video you approve to your TikTok account, with the caption and settings you choose; reads which privacy options and interaction settings your account allows and the post’s processing statusTo publish clips you approve and confirm they were published
video.uploadUploads the video file for a postTo send the clip to TikTok

Information from Google / YouTube, when you connect a YouTube channel

Permission (scope)What ClipPilot reads or doesWhy
youtube.readonlyYour channel ID and name, and the titles of your most recent uploadsTo show which channel you are posting to, and to check whether an interrupted upload already arrived so a video is never posted twice
youtube.uploadUploads a video you approve, with its title, description, tags, visibility and optional publish timeTo publish or schedule clips you approve

Login tokens

When you connect an account, TikTok or Google gives ClipPilot access and refresh tokens. They are saved in a file in ClipPilot’s local data folder, are used only to call the service that issued them, and are never shown again in the app.

3. How information is used

  • To transcribe your videos, suggest clips, render them, and let you review and edit them.
  • To publish, schedule and track the clips you approve on the accounts you connected.
  • To show you your connected accounts and the results of your posts.

Nothing is posted without your approval. ClipPilot does not use your information for advertising, profiling, analytics or any purpose other than the ones above.

4. When information leaves your computer

Only when you use a feature that requires it, and only to the service involved:

  • TikTok: the clip file, its caption and the post settings you choose, when you post to TikTok.
  • YouTube (Google): the clip file, title, description, tags and visibility, when you publish to YouTube.
  • AI providers you turn on (Google Gemini, Groq or a local Ollama): when you use clip finding or title suggestions, the clip’s transcript text and the video title, and for Gemini up to four still frames from the clip. Speech-to-text runs on your own computer.
  • Discord, only if you add a webhook: short messages such as a clip’s title when clips are ready or an upload fails.

Each of these services handles the data under its own privacy policy. We do not sell, rent or trade your information.

5. YouTube API Services

ClipPilot uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove ClipPilot’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Storage and security

All data is stored on the computer running ClipPilot. The app only accepts connections from that same computer (it listens on 127.0.0.1), so it is not reachable from other devices or the internet. Keys and tokens are kept out of source code and version control. As with any software on a personal computer, the security of that data also depends on the security of the computer itself.

7. Keeping and deleting data

  • Data is kept until you delete it. Deleting a video in ClipPilot removes its file, transcript and clips from ClipPilot’s data folder.
  • Disconnecting an account in ClipPilot deletes its saved tokens.
  • You can also revoke access from the platform: in TikTok, go to Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; for Google, use myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Videos already published stay on TikTok or YouTube until you delete them there.

8. Children

ClipPilot is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and is not intended to be used by them.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
